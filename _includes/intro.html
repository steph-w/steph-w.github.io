I am a PhD student candidate at the University of Pennsylvania in the Computer and Information Science department where I am advised by Professor Danaé Metaxa as a member of Penn HCI.

My research employs methods from human-computer interaction, natural language processing, and computational social science to develop human-centered evaluations of AI and algorithmic systems. As a '26 Summer Fellow at Stanford RegLab, I collaborated with a government agency to responsibly bring legal AI tooling into its workflows.

Before starting my PhD, I worked as a software engineer in the tech industry, first at Sisu Data, then at Apple. I earned both my MS in Computer Science and my BS in Symbolic Systems from Stanford University. My work has been generously supported by an AWS gift for research in Trustworthy AI.
